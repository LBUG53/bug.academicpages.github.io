## Scholarship

### Book Project
- ***Anchors of Identity: The Many Paths to Prestige in the American University.*** Invited to submit for peer review, Cornell University Press; revising in response to editorial feedback. Approx. 75,000 words.  
  Comparative institutional history of the Harvard Graduate School of Education, the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education, and Teachers College, Columbia University, showing how each school defended distinct audiences, patrons, and standards of evaluation across four historical inflection points.

### Publications
- “Beyond Disciplinary Prestige: Applied Judgment and the Construction of Public Policy Expertise.” *History of Education Quarterly* 67, no. 1 (2027), forthcoming.

### Under Review
- “Anchors of Identity: Reconsidering Narratives of Academic Drift.” *History of Education Quarterly.* Article-length version of the book’s fourth chapter, on the 1970s budget crises at HGSE, PennGSE, and Teachers College.

### Works in Progress
- *Professional Schools and Their Publics: Local Stakeholders in the American University.* Second book project. Comparative history of professional schools in the American university, centered on the local stakeholders that shaped them. Cases include public policy, social work, and nursing.
- “Local Anchors: How local stakeholders shaped institutional decision-making at UMass Boston, Lesley, and the University of Vermont.” Article on how stakeholders often excluded from institutional narratives shaped education programs at regional institutions. Archival research complete.
- “Making Shared Governance Work: Faculty Senates in Comparative and Historical Perspective.” Comparative study of faculty senates and governance bodies. Interviews complete; historical research in progress.
- “Historical Analysis in Strategic Decision-Making: A Framework for Higher Education.” Article on historically informed decision-making and strategy in higher education. In development.
